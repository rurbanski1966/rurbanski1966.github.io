Privacy Policy: EWSS Email Digest

East West Senior Solutions LLC · Effective November 2, 2026

This policy explains how the EWSS Email Digest application ("the App"), operated by East West Senior Solutions LLC ("we", "us"), handles information. The App is an internal business tool used only by East West Senior Solutions LLC to keep its owner informed about important business email through WhatsApp.

What information the App accesses

With the account owner's authorization, the App connects to the company Gmail account ryan@ewssconsulting.com. It reads email information such as sender, subject, date, and a short portion of message text, only as needed to identify and summarize important business messages.

How the information is used

The App creates short summaries of important new emails and sends them as WhatsApp messages from the company's WhatsApp Business number, +1 443-346-1141, to East West Senior Solutions staff who have agreed to receive them. Replies sent to that WhatsApp number are used only to respond to or follow up on the request.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share email content or personal information with third parties. The only exception is that summary text is transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform for the sole purpose of delivering the digest message. Meta's handling of that data is governed by the WhatsApp Business Policy.

Storage and retention

The App does not keep a separate copy of your email. Digest summaries exist in the WhatsApp conversation and in the company's own systems only as long as needed for business follow-up, and are deleted when no longer needed.

Security

Access credentials for the App are stored securely and used only for the purposes described here. Access is limited to East West Senior Solutions LLC.

Your choices and data deletion

Anyone receiving digests can stop them at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp or by contacting us. To request deletion of any information related to the App, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
